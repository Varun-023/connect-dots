How to Play Connect Dots

Classic 2-Player Strategy on One Device

1
Connect Adjacent Dots

Players take turns connecting two adjacent dots horizontally or vertically. Diagonal lines are not allowed.

2
20-Second Turn Timer

Each player gets 20 seconds to make a move. If the timer expires, the turn automatically passes to the other player.

3
Capture Boxes & Extra Turns

Completing the 4th side of a square captures it in your color (+1 point) and awards you an extra turn with a fresh 20 seconds!

4
Win the Game

When all 25 boxes on the 6x6 grid have been completed, the player with the most captured boxes wins.
